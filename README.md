# Gabriel Chua's Portfolio

Hi! This is my personal portfolio website. I made it for people who want to know something about me, and you can also see my projects there.

I'm a 2nd year Computer Science student at Holy Angel University. My motto is **"Do it, bit-by-bit."**

**Live site:** https://gabrielchua4812-code.github.io/Chua-Portfolio/

## What's inside

The site has six pages:

- **Home** – a short intro and my featured project
- **About** – a bit about me and the tools I use (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Java, and GitHub)
- **Projects** – my projects
- **Case Studies** – my case studies
- **Case Study Preview** – case studies (right now it's only a sample layout)
- **Contacts** – my email, phone, social links, and a form if you want to work with me

## Featured project

**Soilitary** is a gardening website that teaches people how to grow their own food from cheap seeds. You can see it here: https://gabrielchua4812-code.github.io/Soilitary/index.html

## Works on any screen

I designed the site in Figma for three screen sizes:

- Mobile approximately (about 390px)
- Tablet approximately (about 768px)
- Desktop approximately (about 1440px)

## Built with

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript 
- Fonts from Google Fonts: Roboto Mono, Roboto Serif, Righteous, and Radley

## How to run it on your computer

1. Download or clone this repo.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.

That's it. You don't need to install anything.

## Things the JavaScript does

- Filters the cards on the Projects and Case Studies pages as you type in the search bar
- Checks the contact form (name and email) and shows a green or red dot
- Opens your email app with a ready-to-send message when you press Submit
- Scrolls back to the top on the case study page
